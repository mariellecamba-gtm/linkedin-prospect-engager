# How I actually write

**Replace the sample lines below with your own, then delete this line.**

Rules describe a voice. Samples are one. This file is loaded verbatim into the
prompt on every draft, and when a draft disagrees with a rule elsewhere in the
prompt but sounds like these lines, the lines win.

## How to fill this in

1. Open your own LinkedIn profile and scroll your last 30 to 100 posts and
   comments.
2. Paste 15 to 40 lines you actually wrote, verbatim, into the list below. Not
   your best lines. Your normal ones.
3. Include the short ones. Especially the short ones.
4. Do not clean them up. A typo, a lowercase opener and a "lol" are all signal.

Ten honest lines beat forty polished ones. If you have nothing written down, spend
ten minutes typing how you would actually reply to five posts in your feed and
use those.

and a feed full of them is how a prospect works out that a comment was generated.
